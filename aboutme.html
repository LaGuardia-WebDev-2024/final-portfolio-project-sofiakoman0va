about me

my favorite games

Metal Gear Solid 2

I truly love all of the Metal Gear games an insane amount, but MGS2 is my top favorite out of them all. It is just so special and has amazing character relationships and writing.

Tomodachi Life Living the Dream

It is so much fun creating miis and just doing things to them. Feeding them, clothing them, watching them interact with each other; It's genuinely addicting. I spend so much time customizing my characters.

Life is Strange

Life is Strange and Life is Strange 2 are both great games with lovable protagonists. They are notorious for their cringe dialogue and tumblr aesthetic, but I find it so endearing. LiS2 takes the decision-making aspect to a greater level than the first.

my favorite albums

Three Cheers for Sweet Revenge - My Chemical Romance

This album is so amazing and perfect. I can literally listen to it forever, and I have every single song on loop. My favorite songs are Ghost of You, To the End, I'm Not Okay (I Promise), Helena.

I Brought You My Bullets, You Brought Me Your Love
- My Chemical Romance

Can you tell I love MCR? This album is also just gem after gem. My favorite songs (if I had to choose) are Skylines and Turnstiles, Early Sunsets Over Monroeville, and Demolition Lovers.

Oil of Every Pearl's Un-Insides - SOPHIE

This album is beautiful in every way and was so ahead of its time. It never fails to illicite so many emotions out of me. My favorites from this album are It's Okay to Cry, Is It Cold In The Water?, and Immaterial.
